# Sales Analysis Dashboard | Power BI

## 📌 Project Overview
The **Sales Analysis Dashboard** is an interactive business intelligence project developed using Microsoft Power BI to analyze sales performance, profitability, product performance, and business segments. It helps stakeholders monitor key performance indicators (KPIs), compare sales across business categories, and identify opportunities to improve profitability.

## 🎯 Business Objectives
- Analyze overall sales and profit performance.
- Compare profitability across different business segments.
- Identify countries with lower sales or profit performance.
- Evaluate product performance across countries and segments.
- Understand the relationship between sales, discounts, costs, and profit.
- Support data-driven business decisions through interactive visualizations.

## 🛠️ Tools & Technologies
- **Microsoft Power BI** – Dashboard development and visualization
- **Power Query** – Data cleaning and transformation
- **DAX (Data Analysis Expressions)** – Measures and KPI calculations
- **Data Modeling** – Organizing data for reporting and analysis

## 📊 Dataset Description
The project uses sales data containing business and financial information, including:

- Segment
- Country
- Product
- Discount Band
- Units Sold
- Manufacturing Price
- Sale Price
- Gross Sales
- Discounts
- Sales
- COGS (Cost of Goods Sold)
- Profit
- Date
- Month Number
- Month Name
- Year
- Profit Margin

## 📈 Key Dashboard Features
- **KPI Cards:** Summarize important sales and profitability indicators.
- **Sales Analysis:** Compare sales performance across countries and products.
- **Profitability Analysis:** Evaluate profit contribution by segment and product.
- **Country-Level Analysis:** Identify geographical differences in business performance.
- **Segment Comparison:** Understand which customer or business segments contribute to profit.
- **Discount Analysis:** Examine discounts alongside sales and profit performance.
- **Interactive Filters:** Explore data by year, country, segment, product, and other available fields.



## 📌 Key Learnings
- Developed interactive reports using Power BI.
- Practiced data transformation using Power Query.
- Created DAX measures for sales and profitability KPIs.
- Explored trends and comparisons using charts, cards, and slicers.
- Converted raw business data into meaningful visual insights.

## 🚀 Business Value
This dashboard provides a consolidated view of sales and profitability, enabling users to compare business performance, identify areas requiring attention, and make informed decisions using data.

- **Role:** Power BI Developer – Academic/Portfolio Project

## project 2
# IBM HR Analytics Dashboard | Power BI

## 📌 Project Overview
The **IBM HR Analytics Dashboard** is an interactive human resources analytics project developed using Microsoft Power BI. It focuses on employee demographics, workforce distribution, and employee attrition to help HR teams understand workforce patterns and explore factors associated with employee turnover.

The dashboard transforms employee data into interactive visual reports that support HR reporting, workforce planning, and data-driven decision-making.

## 🎯 Business Objectives
- Analyze the total employee population.
- Understand employee attrition and attrition rate.
- Compare employees who left with those who stayed.
- Analyze attrition across job roles and other available employee categories.
- Explore workforce distribution by gender, age, department, and other attributes.
- Identify employee groups that may require further investigation.

## 🛠️ Tools & Technologies
- **Microsoft Power BI** – Interactive dashboards and reports
- **Power Query** – Data cleaning and transformation
- **DAX (Data Analysis Expressions)** – HR metrics and KPI calculations
- **Data Visualization** – Charts, cards, and interactive slicers

## 📊 Dataset Description
The project uses an IBM HR Analytics employee attrition dataset containing employee-level information. Depending on the fields included in the dataset, the analysis covers:

- Employee ID
- Age
- Gender
- Department
- Job Role
- Monthly Income
- Job Satisfaction
- Years at Company
- Years in Current Role
- Work-Life Balance
- Attrition status

## 📈 Key Dashboard Features
- **Total Employee KPI:** Displays the overall employee count.
- **Employee Attrition KPI:** Summarizes the number of employees who left.
- **Attrition Rate:** Shows the proportion of employees who left the organization.
- **Gender Analysis:** Examines workforce distribution by gender.
- **Age Group Analysis:** Compares employee distribution across age groups.
- **Job Role Analysis:** Compares attrition across different job roles.
- **Department Analysis:** Explores workforce and attrition patterns by department.
- **Interactive Slicers:** Filter the dashboard by relevant employee attributes.


## 📌 Key Learnings
- Developed an interactive HR analytics dashboard using Power BI.
- Practiced employee data analysis and transformation.
- Created DAX measures for workforce KPIs.
- Used charts and slicers to analyze attrition patterns.
- Improved understanding of HR metrics and business reporting.
- Practiced presenting employee data in a clear and professional format.

## 🚀 Business Value
The dashboard helps HR professionals monitor employee attrition, understand workforce composition, and identify patterns that may warrant further investigation. It supports workforce reporting and can help guide retention discussions when combined with appropriate business context.
